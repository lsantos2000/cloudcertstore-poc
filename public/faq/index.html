CUSTOMER HELP

Questions answered.
Preparation simplified.

Find quick answers about digital products, checkout, downloads, supported devices, licensing, refunds, and the independent nature of the materials.

How do I purchase an eBook?

Select a title in the catalog and choose “View eBook.” The product page opens on the original storefront, where you can review the current description, preview, price, and checkout options.

How is the digital product delivered?

After a successful checkout on the original store, the download is normally available from the confirmation page and through the receipt email sent by the e-commerce platform.

What should I do if the download email does not arrive?

Check spam and junk folders first. Confirm that you are checking the email address used at checkout. If the message is still missing, use the Contact page and provide the product name and order details.

Can I read the files on a phone or tablet?

Yes. PDF products can be opened on computers, phones, tablets, and compatible e-readers using a current PDF reader.

Are these physical books?

The products linked from this catalog are primarily digital downloads. Selected titles may also have Kindle, paperback, hardcover, or audiobook editions linked from other marketplaces.

Are the guides official or endorsed by certification providers?

No. They are independently published, unofficial, and unaffiliated preparation resources. Provider trademarks are used only to identify the relevant certification.

Do the books contain real exam questions?

No. The books use original, exam-style educational questions created from public exam guides, public documentation, and industry knowledge. They do not contain leaked, recalled, confidential, proprietary, or real exam questions.

Are purchases refundable?

Digital-product sales are generally final once access is provided. Review the preview and the current Refund Policy before purchasing. A separate exam-support guarantee may provide replacement materials or a coupon when its published conditions are met; it is not an automatic cash refund.

Can I share the PDF with another person or team?

No. Standard purchases are licensed to one user and may not be redistributed, uploaded, resold, or shared. Contact the store before purchasing if you need classroom, team, or enterprise rights.

How many times can I download a purchase?

Download limits and link-expiration rules are controlled by the original storefront and may change. Save the file promptly and review the current Terms of Purchase shown at checkout.

Are the materials enough to guarantee a passing score?

No resource can guarantee an exam result. Use the guides with official documentation, hands-on practice, and the current exam blueprint. Certification objectives and services can change.

How do I access earlier purchases?

Use the customer login associated with the original Payhip storefront and the same email address used for checkout. The FAQ on the original store also provides password-reset guidance.

Still need help?

Use the original storefront’s contact form and include the product name and order details.
